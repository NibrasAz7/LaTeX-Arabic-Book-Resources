\section*{Escaped Special Characters}

Dollar: \$100 \\
Percent: 50\% \\

Braces: \{text\} \\
Backslash: \textbackslash \\

Caret: \textasciicircum{} \\
At: user\@example.com

Copyright: \textcopyright \\
Registered: \textregistered \\
Trademark: \texttrademark \\

Euro: \texteuro{}50

\section*{Unicode (Direct Input in XeLaTeX)}

Infinity: $\infty$ \\
