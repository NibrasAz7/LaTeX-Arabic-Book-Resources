\section*{Inline Bibliography Example}

This document demonstrates a manual bibliography built with the
\texttt{thebibliography} environment. No external file is needed.
Reference~\cite{ref1} discusses quantum mechanics, while reference~\cite{ref2}
covers general relativity.

\begin{thebibliography}{9}

\bibitem{ref1}
Dirac, P. A. M.
\textit{The Principles of Quantum Mechanics}.
Oxford University Press, 1930.

\bibitem{ref2}
Einstein, A.
\textit{The Meaning of Relativity}.
Princeton University Press, 1922.

\bibitem{ref3}
Feynman, R. P.
``Space-Time Approach to Non-Relativistic Quantum Mechanics.''
\textit{Reviews of Modern Physics}, 20(2):367--387, 1948.

\end{thebibliography}
